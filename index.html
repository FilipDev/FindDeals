Multi-Source Architecture: Live flyer pricing via Flipp (backflipp.wishabi.com) merged with your own Price Book (prices you save per store, stored locally) and cross-run price history trends. Postal & GPS geocoding via Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), Photon (Komoot), and Zippopotam.us. Physical store branch discovery combines Flipp Store Metadata, a 3-mirror Overpass QL cluster (overpass-api.de, kumi.systems, mail.ru), Photon Spatial POI Search, Nominatim Bounded Search, and optionally Google Places API (your own key, set in ⚙️ Settings) with strict retail POI validation.
Location trust engine: self-reported store coordinates are cross-verified against OpenStreetMap, area-centroid leaks are detected and discarded, and remaining unverified pins are reverse-address-checked via Photon. Any branch — or an entire chain — can be excluded from routing on the map or in store cards; exclusions are remembered per postal code. Driving distance matrices and turn-by-turn road geometry use OSRM (router.project-osrm.org) and FOSSGIS OSRM (routing.openstreetmap.de).
Unofficial tool; not affiliated with Flipp, Google, or any retail chain. Always verify in-store unit sizes, loyalty card requirements, and quantity limits.
